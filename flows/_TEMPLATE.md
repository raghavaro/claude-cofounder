Six steps, roughly 30 minutes. Output is a price you can say out loud without
flinching, and the list of things that have to be true for it to hold.

Run one step per turn. See the pacing section in `SKILL.md`.

Open by telling them the shape: what the module produces, and which step is the
one that matters. If a step depends on an earlier module, say what it needs and
offer the short version of that module if the brief doesn't have it.
